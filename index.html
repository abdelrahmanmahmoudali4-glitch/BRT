Bus Rapid Transit

The Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) is a modern and intelligent public transport system operating on the Cairo Ring Road. It offers high-speed, efficient service through dedicated lanes, smart stations, internal and external security cameras, and an advanced online booking system.
The BRT system is designed to save time, reduce congestion and pollution, and provide a safer, smarter travel experience through real-time tracking, automated alerts, and digital payment options.
